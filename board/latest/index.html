Key Takeaways

Executive view

Heirloom closed Q1 2026 with a +$11.6k operating surplus on the back of +73% year-over-year Q1 giving growth — from roughly $66k in Q1 2025 to $114k this quarter — and a peak count of 113 active givers, the highest in church history. The balance sheet carries zero long-term debt, with approximately 12.4 months of runway on internal giving alone if every dollar of external partner support were to disappear overnight. The Local Missions escrow is fully funded at $11.2k, set aside from the 10–11% missional allocation of internal tithes and offerings, keeping the SPT designated-fund requirement covered.
